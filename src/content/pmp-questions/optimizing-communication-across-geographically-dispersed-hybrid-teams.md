Your global project team consists of on-site business analysts and a remote software development team operating in a timezone eight hours ahead. Stakeholders complain they are not getting timely status updates, while the remote development team reports being overwhelmed by unscheduled meeting requests during their evening hours.

What is the project manager's best response to resolve this friction?

* A. Direct the remote development team to adjust their working hours to fully match the home office timezone.
* B. Review and update the Communications Management Plan to establish defined core collaboration hours and asynchronous update channels.
* C. Cancel all virtual meetings and require all status updates to be sent exclusively via daily email reports.
* D. Appoint a co-project manager to oversee operations in the remote timezone.
