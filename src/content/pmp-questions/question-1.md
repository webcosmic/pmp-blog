### The Scenario
During a weekly status meeting, a key stakeholder casually requests a minor feature change that was not in the original scope. They claim it's "no big deal."

### The Question
What is the **first** thing a Project Manager should do?

A) Add it immediately to keep the stakeholder happy.
B) Politely decline and refer to the Scope Statement.
C) Evaluate the impact on the project's triple constraints.
D) Update the Project Management Plan.

### The Answer
**Correct Answer: C** *Humorous Tip:* In the PMP universe, never just "do it" or "say no" without doing your homework first. Assess the damage before you open the door!
