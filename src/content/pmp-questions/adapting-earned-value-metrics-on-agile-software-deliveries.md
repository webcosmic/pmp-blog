You are managing a software development project with a fixed-price budget. During your mid-project review, you calculate that the project’s Cost Performance Index (CPI) is 0.85 and the Schedule Performance Index (SPI) is 1.15. The team is currently utilizing two-week sprints.

How should you interpret these metrics and communicate them to key stakeholders?

* A. The project is under budget and ahead of schedule; no corrective actions are needed.
* B. The project is over budget but ahead of schedule; collaborate with the Product Owner to optimize backlog prioritization and review sprint burn rates.
* C. The project is over budget and behind schedule; request a formal budget increase from the project sponsor immediately.
* D. Standard EVM formulas do not apply to agile software frameworks and should be omitted from stakeholder status updates.
