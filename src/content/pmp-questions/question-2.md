### The Scenario
Right in the middle of a critical sprint, your lead database administrator suddenly goes on emergency medical leave for two weeks. There is no backup DBA assigned to the project, and a critical database migration is scheduled for this Friday. 

### The Question
What is the **first** thing the Project Manager should do?

A) Postpone the database migration by two weeks until the lead DBA returns.
B) Review the Resource Management Plan and the Risk Register for pre-defined strategies.
C) Ask another developer on the team to guess their way through the migration.
D) Escalate the issue to the Project Sponsor immediately to request external hiring budget.

### The Answer
**Correct Answer: B** *Humorous Tip:* In the PMP universe, never panic-escalate or guess. Always look at your "books" (the plans) first. The answers to your life's problems are usually buried in a risk response plan you wrote six months ago and forgot about!
