A lead cloud architect assigned to your high-priority digital transformation initiative is suddenly reassigned by their functional manager to a corporate emergency. The sprint velocity drops by 40%. 

What is the best immediate action for the agile project manager?

* A. Request the project sponsor issue a formal directive to return the architect.
* B. Collaborate with the remaining team members to re-estimate the backlog velocity and adjust expectations.
* C. Halt all current phase deployments until a replacement resource of equal caliber is onboarded.
* D. File an official complaint with the PMO regarding functional resource hoarding.
